Atlas Audiobook Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Atlas Audiobook Companion ("the App") is developed by David Greenland. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All processing — audio conversion, metadata editing, chapter management — occurs entirely on your Mac and never leaves your device.

File Access

The App requests access to audio files and folders you explicitly select. These files are read locally for conversion purposes only and are never uploaded or shared.

Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
