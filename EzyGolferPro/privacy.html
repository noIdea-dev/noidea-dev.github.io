Privacy Policy

EzyGolfer Pro — Effective 29 September 2026

EzyGolfer Pro is an iOS golf scoring app built by David Greenland. This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is stored, and what is never collected.

What We Collect

EzyGolfer Pro collects only the minimum information needed for the app to function:

  • Location (GPS). The app requests access to your device location while the app is open, solely to calculate distances from your current position to greens and hazards during a round. Location data is used in real time and never stored or transmitted.
  • Golf data you enter. Round scores, player names, and handicaps are stored locally on your device using CoreData. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information is sent to any server.
  • There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.
  • There are no analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party trackers of any kind.
  • There is no advertising.

In-App Purchases

EzyGolfer Pro offers optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never see or store your payment information. All purchase transactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Export

You can export all your data as a JSON file at any time from the app's Settings screen. That file is saved directly to your device and shared only if you choose to share it. We have no access to exported files.

Data Retention

All data is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data. We hold no copies of your data on any server.

Children's Privacy

EzyGolfer Pro does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app collects no personal data from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact
