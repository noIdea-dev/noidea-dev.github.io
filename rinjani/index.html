About Rinjani

Rinjani is a private chat for our Lombok circle of friends, expats and Indonesian colleagues. Instead of one big chat where everything gets buried, topics live in their own groups that only their members can see, and any member is one tap away for a private message. It works on iPhone, Android and Mac, and it is free with no ads.

What you can do: join topic groups, send private messages, get notifications, and keep writing when the signal drops: messages wait and send when you are back online. The first time on a device you sign in with a code sent to your email, so there is no password to remember.

Coming: English and Indonesian translation on your own phone, plus search and message tools.

How it was made: David Greenland designed and built Rinjani with an AI development partner. One shared Swift codebase makes the iPhone, Mac and Android apps (Android through Skip), and messages are kept on private servers in Singapore. The app icon is David's own artwork.

Before you start

You need an invitation. Someone in the group invites your email address and an admin approves it. If you haven't been invited, ask a member.

1. Get the app

Android (APK) — download iPhone (TestFlight) — coming soon Mac (download)

2. Android install steps

Thanks for agreeing to help test the Rinjani app. Follow the steps below to set it up on your Android phone.

  1. Approve your phone (once). Google now asks for this in Indonesia. David will send you a personal link. Open it on your phone, confirm you know and trust David, follow the screens, then copy the long code it gives you and send it back to David. Don't retype it.
  2. Download. Use Chrome. Tap the Android button above and download Rinjani.apk (about 85 MB).
  3. Allow the install. Your phone will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details and then Install anyway. (Rinjani is a private app, not from a store.)
  5. Open Rinjani.

Help for your phone brand

Menu names differ a little between phones and Android versions. If yours doesn't match, send David a screenshot.

Samsung (Galaxy)
  • Download with Chrome or Samsung Internet. When asked, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  • If the install is blocked and mentions Auto Blocker: open Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker and switch it off while you install. You can switch it back on afterwards.
  • If Google Play Protect warns: More details → Install anyway.
Oppo and Realme
  • Download with Chrome, then tap Install. When asked, tap Settings and allow your browser to Install unknown apps.
  • Your phone may run a security check and show a warning, sometimes with a short countdown. Wait, then choose Install anyway or Continue.
  • If the install fails the first time, tap the downloaded file and try again.
Vivo and iQOO
  • Download with Chrome, then tap Install. When asked, allow your browser under Install unknown apps (often in Settings → Security or Special app access).
  • A virus or security scan may appear. Choose Continue or Install to go on.
Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO
  • Download with Chrome, then tap Install. When asked, allow Chrome to Install unknown apps (Settings → Privacy protection → Special permissions).
  • The phone's Security app may scan the file and warn about a risk because Rinjani is not from a store. Choose Install anyway.

3. Sign in (first time only)

  1. Type the email address we invited.
  2. Tap Email me a code.
  3. Open the email from Rinjani, find the 6-digit code, type it in the app.
  4. Choose the name other members will see. Allow notifications.

No password to remember. You only need a code the first time on each phone or computer, because Rinjani keeps you signed in after that. You will be asked for a new code only if you sign out, reinstall the app or set up a new device.

Tentang Rinjani

Rinjani adalah obrolan pribadi untuk lingkaran kita di Lombok: teman, ekspat, dan rekan kerja dari Indonesia. Alih-alih satu obrolan besar tempat semuanya tenggelam, setiap topik punya grupnya sendiri yang hanya bisa dilihat anggotanya, dan setiap anggota hanya satu ketukan jauhnya untuk pesan pribadi. Rinjani berjalan di iPhone, Android, dan Mac, gratis dan tanpa iklan.

Yang bisa Anda lakukan: bergabung di grup topik, mengirim pesan pribadi, menerima notifikasi, dan tetap menulis saat sinyal hilang: pesan menunggu dan terkirim saat Anda kembali online. Pertama kali di sebuah perangkat, Anda masuk dengan kode yang dikirim ke email, jadi tidak ada kata sandi yang perlu diingat.

Segera hadir: terjemahan Inggris dan Indonesia langsung di ponsel Anda, serta pencarian dan alat pesan.

Bagaimana dibuat: David Greenland merancang dan membangun Rinjani bersama mitra pengembangan AI. Satu basis kode Swift menghasilkan aplikasi iPhone, Mac, dan Android (Android lewat Skip), dan pesan disimpan di server pribadi di Singapura. Ikon aplikasi adalah karya seni David sendiri.

2. Langkah pasang di Android

Terima kasih telah bersedia ikut menguji aplikasi Rinjani. Ikuti langkah-langkah di bawah ini untuk memasangnya di ponsel Android Anda.

  1. Setujui ponsel Anda (satu kali). Google kini meminta ini di Indonesia. David akan mengirim tautan pribadi. Buka tautan itu di ponsel Anda, konfirmasi bahwa Anda mengenal dan memercayai David, ikuti layarnya, lalu salin kode panjang yang muncul dan kirimkan kembali kepada David. Jangan diketik ulang.
  2. Unduh. Gunakan Chrome. Ketuk tombol Android di atas dan unduh Rinjani.apk (sekitar 85 MB).
  3. Izinkan pemasangan. Ponsel akan berkata tidak dapat memasang aplikasi dari sumber ini. Ketuk Pengaturan, aktifkan Izinkan dari sumber ini, kembali, lalu ketuk Instal.
  4. Jika Google Play Protect menampilkan peringatan, ketuk Detail lainnya lalu Tetap instal. (Rinjani adalah aplikasi pribadi, bukan dari toko aplikasi.)
  5. Buka Rinjani.

3. Masuk (hanya pertama kali)

  1. Ketik alamat email yang kami undang.
  2. Ketuk Email me a code (Kirim kode lewat email).
  3. Buka email dari Rinjani, cari kode 6 angka, lalu ketik di aplikasi.
  4. Pilih nama yang akan dilihat anggota lain. Izinkan notifikasi.

Tidak ada kata sandi yang perlu diingat. Kode hanya diperlukan saat pertama kali di setiap ponsel atau komputer, karena setelah itu Rinjani membuat Anda tetap masuk. Kode baru hanya diminta jika Anda keluar, memasang ulang aplikasi, atau memakai perangkat baru.
