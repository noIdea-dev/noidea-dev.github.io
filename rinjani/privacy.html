Rinjani

Privacy Policy

In short

Rinjani is a private, invitation-only chat. We collect only what is needed to run it. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to invite you and to send your sign-in code.
  • Your display name, shown to the people you share groups with.
  • Your messages (text) and which groups and conversations you belong to.
  • A notification token for your device, so a new message can reach your phone when the app is closed.
  • A sign-in session, so you stay signed in.

Rinjani does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Who can see it

Group messages are visible to the members of that group. Private messages are visible to the two people in them. A group that you are not in is hidden from you completely. The administrator who runs Rinjani can see who belongs to each group and can remove a member or delete a group.

Translation

Translation happens on your own device using the translation features built into your phone. Your messages are not sent to any translation service.

Services that help us run Rinjani

  • Supabase stores the accounts and messages (servers in Singapore).
  • Brevo sends the invitation and sign-in code emails.
  • Apple (and, on Android, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) deliver the notifications.
  • If you test the iPhone app through Apple TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and usage information with the developer, as described in TestFlight's own terms.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept until a group is deleted or you ask for yours to be removed. Backups of the database are kept for up to 30 days. Deleting a group hides it from everyone straight away.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to be removed from Rinjani altogether, by writing to the address below. Rinjani is for adults and is not directed at children.
