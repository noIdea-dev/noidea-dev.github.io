EzyGolfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

EzyGolfer ("the App") is developed by David Greenland. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter — rounds, scores, course information — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework and never leaves your device.

Location Data

The App requests access to your location solely to provide GPS distance measurements to the green while you are actively playing a round. Location data is used in real time and is never stored, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Data Storage

All app data is stored on-device only. You may export your data at any time via Settings → Export Data and delete it by removing the App from your device.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
